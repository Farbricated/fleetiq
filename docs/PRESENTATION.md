# FleetIQ Hackathon Presentation

## Slide 1: PROBLEM
**Title**: The Heavy Equipment Blind Spot
**Text**:
- Millions lost annually to idle assets and mismatched allocations.
- Managers have raw data, but lack automated intelligence to surface what matters.

**Speaker Notes**:
"Today, construction and rental fleets hemorrhage money simply because equipment sits idle on one site while another desperately needs it. Managers are drowning in telematics data, but they don't have the time to manually cross-reference utilization against upcoming demand. FleetIQ solves this gap."

**Anticipated Q&A**:
*Q: How is this different from existing telematics dashboards?*
*A: Traditional dashboards just show you where an asset is. FleetIQ actively flags what's wrong and recommends how to fix it based on upcoming demand.*

---

## Slide 2: SOLUTION
**Title**: FleetIQ: Intelligence, Not Just Information
**Text**:
- An active engine that predicts demand and flags underutilization.
- **Differentiator**: Human-in-the-loop decision making, not opaque autopilot.

**Speaker Notes**:
"FleetIQ is an active intelligence layer. It doesn't just display data; it uses deterministic rules and forecasting to recommend asset reallocations. But crucially, it keeps the human in the loop. We don't want an AI re-routing heavy machinery autonomously—we want it teeing up the perfect decision for a manager to approve."

**Anticipated Q&A**:
*Q: Why not just automate the reallocation completely?*
*A: Accountability. As we documented in our ADR, a fully autonomous AI Copilot steals user agency and introduces hallucination risk. Fleet managers need to trust the system, and that means they must retain final approval.*

---

## Slide 3: THE DEMO (Spot, Explain, Act)
**Title**: The Intelligence Workflow
**Text**:
- **SPOT**: Dashboard surfaces high-risk idle assets organically.
- **EXPLAIN**: Asset 360 gives deterministic natural language insight.
- **ACT**: Manager approves reallocations instantly from the queue.

**Speaker Notes**:
"Let's look at the workflow. First, we SPOT the issue: the dashboard surfaces assets with critical underutilization. Second, we EXPLAIN: opening the asset provides a deterministic natural language summary of why it was flagged, powered by our backend rule engine. Third, we ACT: the manager reviews the recommendation and approves it seamlessly."

**Anticipated Q&A**:
*Q: Is the natural language summary generated by an LLM?*
*A: No. It's a deterministic string generated by our backend rule engine. We chose this to ensure high impact without the latency or hallucination risk of an LLM during the demo.*

---

## Slide 4: THE DEMO (Predict)
**Title**: Forecasting Demand
**Text**:
- **WMA Engine**: Weighted Moving Average predicts future site needs.
- **Data-Driven Candidates**: Ranks the best reallocation matches.

**Speaker Notes**:
"Before a manager approves a move, they need to PREDICT the outcome. Our backend uses a Weighted Moving Average to forecast demand based on historical snapshot data. The system then automatically ranks the best allocation candidates, ensuring that managers have data-driven options before they commit."

**Anticipated Q&A**:
*Q: Where is the What-If simulation you mentioned in your earlier scope?*
*A: We chose to remove the What-If simulation stub. Building a live dynamic engine risked breaking the core flow, and showing a static mock undermined the authenticity of the rest of the application. We prioritized shipping a robust, production-ready WMA engine over a mocked UI.*

---

## Slide 5: IMPACT & RESULTS (Prove)
**Title**: Proving the Value
**Text**:
- Actions are tracked directly to business outcomes.
- **Metric**: Projected Utilization Improvement.
- **Provenance**: Strictly labeled ILLUSTRATIVE ESTIMATE or REAL.

**Speaker Notes**:
"Finally, we PROVE the value. Once an action is taken, FleetIQ tracks the projected business impact—like utilization improvement scores. We are obsessive about data provenance. Every metric on the impact page tells you exactly whether it's a REAL measurement from the field or an ILLUSTRATIVE ESTIMATE of future gains."

**Anticipated Q&A**:
*Q: Where do the impact numbers come from?*
*A: They are currently derived from the allocation candidate scoring algorithm. Because the action hasn't fully played out in the real world yet, they are strictly branded as illustrative estimates in the UI.*

---

## Slide 6: CLOSER & TECH STACK
**Title**: Built for Scale, Grounded in Reality
**Text**:
- **Stack**: React 18, FastAPI, PostgreSQL (Dockerized).
- **Intentional Scope Cuts**: Map View & AI Copilot deferred.
- **Status**: Production-ready.

**Speaker Notes**:
"FleetIQ is built on a production-ready stack: React, FastAPI, and Postgres, fully Dockerized for cross-platform deployment. You'll notice we didn't include a Map View or an AI Copilot. That was intentional discipline. A map view is fragile and redundant to our tabular intelligence, and an AI Copilot undermines the human-in-the-loop trust we are building. FleetIQ is ready to deploy today."

**Anticipated Q&A**:
*Q: Why cut the map view if it's a fleet tracking app?*
*A: A sorted asset table achieves the 'Spot' beat with zero layout risk. A map adds no narrative value that our intelligence dashboard doesn't already solve better.*
