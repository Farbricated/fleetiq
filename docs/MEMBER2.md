# FleetIQ Member 2 Responsibilities

As Member 2 of the FleetIQ project, my core focus is transforming the platform from a reactive dashboard into a proactive decision engine. 

## Ownership Areas
- **Phase 8 (Allocation Intelligence):** Scoring and ranking assets to meet forecasted demand while adhering to risk constraints.
- **Phase 9 (Recommendation Engine):** Packaging candidates into actionable recommendations with clear rationale.
- **Phase 10 (Decision / Action / Impact):** Managing the lifecycle of recommendations (approval, rejection, execution) and tracking their operational and financial impact.

## Key Principles
- **No Hardcoding:** Scenarios adapt dynamically based on data, not special-case logic (e.g., no hardcoding EQX1007 to S003).
- **Auditability:** All actions (approvals, rejections) must be linked to a specific user and timestamped.
- **Explainability:** Allocation scores must provide clear, JSON-based reasoning detailing how sub-scores were calculated.
